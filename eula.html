Internal pilot terms

Operator: Mike Wojcik (GitHub account: likwidgames). Scope: the local Relayboard hosting pilot. Effective November 1, 2026.

Permission to use the pilot

The operator permits designated pilot participants to use the local Relayboard hosting integration for internal evaluation, with repositories and hosting projects they are authorized to manage. This permission is limited, non-transferable and revocable by the operator.

Control over work and releases

Participants must use the owner decisions and access controls provided by the pilot. Connecting an account or approving setup does not authorize a Production release. A Production release requires the owner’s separate decision for the exact reviewed candidate.

Source and content

These pilot terms do not transfer ownership of repositories, source files or task content to the operator. Participants must have permission to supply the content and connect the accounts used by the pilot.

Experimental service

The pilot is experimental and provided on an as-is basis. It may fail, pause or require operator assistance. The operator makes no uptime, support response or fitness commitment for this internal evaluation. Participants should retain their own source history and review proposed changes.

Provider accounts and costs

GitHub, Vercel and any separately enabled AI provider remain governed by their own terms and account settings. Connecting the pilot does not authorize new purchases, changed billing limits or additional usage beyond the owner’s approved scope.

Ending access

The operator may end the pilot. Participants may stop using it and revoke provider access in the relevant provider settings. Disconnecting a connection in Relayboard preserves existing hosted resources and saved task history; it does not delete GitHub secrets or provider records automatically.

Contact

Contact the operator through the existing pilot support channel. These terms cover internal evaluation; commercial customer terms require separate preparation and approval.
